Windows updates can sometimes be a real pain, like in the case of this HP laptop that just came into my shop. the owner of this system said the computer was working fine one night, and they went to actually shut it down. The next morning, they opened it up to find that there was an update pinning that installed onto their computer that completely took away their ability to connect to Wi-Fi. Like, the option doesn't even exist anymore. Well, if you ever get an issue like this, typically this is associated with a bad driver update or something interfering with the driver for the actual Wi-Fi adapter. To fix this issue, we first need to right click on the Windows icon and select Device Manager, where we can then see that there's a suspicious triangle next to the Wi-Fi adapter, which, after right clicking and opening up the properties, shows the device was failing to start. Which, fortunately, could be fixed by just going over to the driver section and reloading the driver back on the device by selecting the Update Driver option, followed by the manual option, and selecting the appropriate driver for the machine. After doing that option to connect, the Wi Fi is now back, and we are now back to having internet access on the system. So let's go and get it back over to our customer.
