Normally when you see this really large dip in the amount of time that the system actually starts up, this typically can only be caused by something like heat. The stock mounting hardware for AM5 is still installed and needs to actually be removed for a proper install. Meaning that the CPU was never being cooled and likely it was just instantly Thermal throttleing and just protecting itself by shutting off.
