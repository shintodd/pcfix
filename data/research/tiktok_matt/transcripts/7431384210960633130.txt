A clicking sound coming from your PC is one of the last things you ever want to hear because it either means you have a damaged fan or in this case, a failed hard drive. And the mechanical drive inside this PC was in such bad shape that it caused the PC to not even display anything out if it was connected, as it caused the system just to fail to post every time. Which for some reason on these MSI boards will actually hang the VGA diagnostic light. Which was kind of weird for a hard drive issue. But after just unplugging the drive, the PC was just back to booting just fine. And after doing a further evaluation on that hard drive, that drive is an absolute goner because when I docked it into a test system it actually clicked a few times and then shut right off, signifying an internal failure. And the crazy part is is that this PC is only a year or two old, so it shouldn't have failed this soon. Until you pull back the sticker which shows this drive was actually manufactured in 2,010 and that the pre built manufacturer peripherals who built the system put in a 14 year old refurbished drive into this supposedly new system. So no wonder it failed so soon. Fortunately our customer didn't use this drive very much, so the data loss is going to be fairly minimal, but they still wanted the extra space because The boot drive is only 240 gigabytes. So we can take this brand new 1 terabyte drive, plug that in and get it installed in the system, initialize it in Windows, and just like that, the system is now back up and running and ready for a customer to come pick up.
