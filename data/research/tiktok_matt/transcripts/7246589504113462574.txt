About 5 months ago I found, bought, and installed the absolute cheapest custom water cooling kit you can buy. Ended up costing less than $100 on Amazon and actually performed quite well during my testing. However, something I wanted to do was leave the system connected to test the long term reliability of such a cheap kit. And after keeping the system off to the side of my shop for some time, I pulled him out to see that the coloring of the tubing had turned this light blue color. Which is really odd because the coolant that we put in the system was completely clear. And I honestly don't think it was a coolant's fault either because the liquid in the reservoir, CPU, and GPU blocks are completely clear. With all this said, the system surprisingly still runs great, but this is definitely a downside of using such cheap tubing.
