A customer just brought in this Lenovo laptop saying that their system won't turn on, so let's get it fixed for them. After doing a quick test to confirm the issue, I noticed that the LED indicator for charging was stuck on this solid white light. And when I plugged in the battery pack that allows you to see the USBC output wattage, it noticed that the system was actually pulling no power from the wall. So this is normally a good indication of a failing laptop battery. So let's go ahead and get this tested and then possibly swapped out. To do this, we first need to remove the bottom cover off the laptop and then unplug the battery from there. Then I was thinking curious if the system might boot up if we just have power from the wall, and after doing a quick test, it did. So in this case, I went ahead and plugged the battery pack back in to go ahead and see if we just needed the power reset, but it ended up getting no change from before. So it looks like in this case we do have a bad battery, and we can install this brand new one here. To do this, all we have to do is unscrew the old battery and then place this new one in, screw it down, and then get it plugged in. From there we can reassemble the laptop. And after firing it up, it loaded right Up into Windows without any issues. And we can now get this working laptop back to the customer.
