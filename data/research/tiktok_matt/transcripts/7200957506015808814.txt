A viewer? Can you cool a CPU with a cup full of thermal paste? Well, in order to test this, I took 3 tubs of thermal paste and scooped out the thermal paste into a copper cup and then placed that copper cup on a CPU with some thermal paste. After firing up the system, the CPU was sitting around 59 degrees Celsius at idle, and after trying to run a CPU stress test, the cup with thermal paste only lasted 35 seconds before the CPU reached 100 degrees Celsius. So as expected, you can't cool a CPU with the cup with thermal paste. But what should we try next? Let me know in the comments down below.
