This GTX 6 60 for a massage. By far the most destroyed card of our entire G V lot we recently purchased on eBay, so let's take it apart and see how much damage was actually done to the board. Now with the cooler removed, we can see that the board wasn't in too bad a shape all things considered, as the bend on the board was mainly resolved by just removing a support bracket. However, there were two noticeable board related issues. First was the fan connector, which was completely smashed down as a result of being under the area of impact on the cooler. Second was that this choke was chipped, which likely would have rendered the card unbootable even if we were able to straighten the board out perfectly. Do you think this card could be repaired?
