A customer just brought in this Dell laptop saying that their batteries seem to be dying significantly faster than ever before. So let's examine it and get it fixed. First in order to verify the issue, Iran dell's built in diagnostic utility which found an issue with the battery showing that was only operating at 56 of its usable capacity. So it looks like we just need to replace the battery. In order to do this, we first had to remove the back cover off the laptop and then remove the battery and replace it with a brand new one. From there, the system then could be reassembled and with the new battery in place, I fired up the system and ran the double diagnostic tool again, where the battery showed up in perfect health. With the battery now operating properly, we can now get the system back to the customer.
