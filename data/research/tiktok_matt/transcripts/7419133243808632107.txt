What's the difference between Intel EVO and Intel vpro? Well, they're both certification platforms put on by Intel, but the main difference is one's for consumers and one's for businesses. The Intel EVO program is basically the premium Ultrabook lineup of laptops with Intel CPUs. They have to have great battery life, they have to be portable, great connectivity, and a bunch of other minor requirements. On the other hand, Intel vpro is all about the management and security side of a laptop, making it easier for IT administration. One of the main security differences for Intel V Pro is the fact that it has to have on board security that goes beyond the actual operating system level. This means that IT administrators can manage that security and set up certain profiles to protect from additional threats that you may not normally see on the consumer side that would target something like the BIOS or other aspects of the back into the computer that could open the system up for a vulnerability. And then the other thing that's huge for IT departments is the active management that has to be built in. Basically, on a traditional like, management platform, you can have a laptop be managed with a tool, but it doesn't do anything on the back end of the computer, or if the operating system is not working, or if you happen to have a laptop that's off, there's nothing you can do. But with the Intel vpro system, You can actually turn on a computer remotely as well as access it from a remote location, even when it's not working properly. It makes the lives of a system administrator a lot easier, and it's something that is vital in business environments. You may have in points deployed all over the world. And that's why there's a massive premium if you end up buying an Intel vpro laptop. But for most consumers, those features really don't matter. So if you do want a certification platform to make sure you're getting the best ultrabooks, look at the Intel EVO program. And you can kind of forget about vpro unless you're a system administrator.
