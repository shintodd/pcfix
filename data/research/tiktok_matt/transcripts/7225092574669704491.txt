Customer just brought in this gaming PC saying that their mouse and keyboard have been constantly cutting out, so let's get that fixed for them. In order to do this, I first fired up Windows and immediately noticed that my mouse and keyboard would just stop working every 30 seconds or so. And after opening up Device Manager to see what was going on, I realized that the Bluetooth adapter, keyboard, and mouse just kept refreshing like they were being reconnected over and over again. At that point I was thinking maybe it was an issue with the Bluetooth card as that also runs over USB. And after unplugging it, I realized that the system actually had a splitter, which I've had issues with in the past with USB 2.0. And after disconnecting the splitter and plugging in the devices manually, the issue went away. So it looks like it was just a bad splitter. And after replacing that and putting the system back together, we can now get this back to the customer.
