After pulling out the old one, we can go and slot that into place, plug in all the power cables to the motherboard, and then at that point install our brand new GPU and plug in its power cable. The system is now upgraded. But a good rule of thumb before we do anything the second you upgrade your GPU, go and check if there are any driver updates to ensure your card is running at its peak.
