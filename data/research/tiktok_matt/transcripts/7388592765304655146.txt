This Samsung Galaxy Flip 5 has experienced a black glob of death where about 80% of the entire main screen is completely unreadable and the touchscreen has also stopped working all together. However, the owner of this Flip 5 wanted to see if we could possibly still recover their data without replacing the entire screen to avoid the overpriced cost of these displays. Unfortunately, the Flip 5, unlike the Flip 4, supports external displays on USB C hubs. So all we have to do in this case is first unlock the phone, which we can still do using the front screen that still happens to work, and then connect the USB C hub over to the phone that has power connected, an HDMI port that goes to a monitor, USB receiver for a mouse for input, and lastly a storage device to back up all the data. We can then use the monitor and mouse to go and navigate on the phone to find settings and then look for the Smart Switch option and pick one of my favorite options that Sam added a while back, which is the ability to back up their options to a separate external storage device. This then allows us to copy every single one of the files from our customers old phone to this flash drive and after letting that run for a few hours, all the data is backed up so our customer can then now plug this flash drive into their brand New phone and restore all their files on the replacement.
