A customer just brought in this HP laptop claiming that all of a sudden their touch screen just stopped working. So let's get it fixed for them. After doing a quick test and notepad using the on screen keyboard, I was able to notice that there was clearly no input from the touch screen at all. So in most cases like this, this is often driver related. So I went into Device Manager on the system where I was able to find that the system failed to start up the touchscreen interface device. So just to be safe, I went ahead and updated the touchscreen driver. And after doing so, when you know what, the touchscreen came right back up. And with the system now working, we can now get this HP laptop back to the customer.
