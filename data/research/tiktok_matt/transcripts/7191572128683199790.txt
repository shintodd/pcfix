this is the Nvidia GTX 295, the fastest GPU for gaming in 2009. what made this GPU so special outside of its performance was the fact that it wasn't one GPU. rather it was 2 GPUs and one actually working together. but instead of both chips being on the same PCB like other dual GPU cards at the time, Nvidia decided to make 2 different Pcbs with separate GPUs. so you can almost think of it like a 2 way SLI setup in a single dual slot card. the GPUs together ran at 576 Mhz and had a total of 1 792 MB of memory. this was truly a beast of a GPU at the time. but do you think I can run any games today? let me know in the comments down below.
