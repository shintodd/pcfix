A few weeks back, we serviced this HP laptop, and the customer said that they're running into a new issue after the repair where the system was failing to load in the operating system. And after turning the system on to see what was going on, the first time I noticed that there was a overheating air for the CPU. So in order to fix this issue, we're gonna have to take this system apart and inspect the fan and possibly repase the CPU. This system is at least 8 years old, and it's definitely time for that to be done. In order to do this, I first removed the battery, followed by every screw and cable that was preventing the bottom cover from being released. And once that was off, I unscrewed the fan and took off the cooling assembly, where I was able to clean off the old Thermal paste and put some brand new Arctic. Luckily, in this case, the system fan appeared to be in really good condition, and it was just a little dusty, but some compressed air was able to take care of that. From there, I put the system back together and the system was back up and booting, and it was no longer getting into overheating CPU air. So with this system now fixed, we can now get it back to the customer.
