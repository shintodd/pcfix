I recently upgraded my Intel stock cooler by adding a 10 000 RPM fan to it and I got some great results. but I wanted to see if flipping the fan around from the normal down draft position to an up draft position would affect performance, so I swapped the orientation. after doing so, I saw a slight increase in performance as the idle temperatures went from 25 to 23 degrees Celsius and under load the CPU was sitting at 48 degrees Celsius compared to 52 before. although we did see a temperature improvement, the amount of noise being produced seemed to go up a little bit with the new configuration.
