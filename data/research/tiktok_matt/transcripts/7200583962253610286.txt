A while back I upgraded the Intel stock cooler with a 6000 RPM fan and we saw some great results, but I thought we could take it up to the next level, so I bought this 17 000 RPM fan and attached it to the Intel stock cooler using some zip ties. After doing so, temperatures at idle were sitting around 23 degrees Celsius, and after running a CPU stress test we were sitting in a low 50s, clearly outperforming the regular Stockler. But in order to get this great performance, it came at the expense of my ear.
