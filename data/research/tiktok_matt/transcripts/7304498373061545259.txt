Sometimes laptop manufactures choices and design just really frustrate me. Such as this one over here, as this is a massive 17 inch laptop with plenty of space. But for some reason lenova decided there to be smart to only have one stick of removal RAM with the rest of it being soldered to the board. Okay, well you may think that's not all that bad, but here's the issue the second that the soldered memory fails, guess what? Your motherboard is pretty much garbage as it's just not replaceable. The cost of a replacement of these boards means that your system is pretty much garbage. This is just one way that manufacturers are slowly taking away the ability to repair laptops. Not just from shops like mine, but from consumers who could easily swap out their RAM before.
