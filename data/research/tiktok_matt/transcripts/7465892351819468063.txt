If you ever have to install a CPU cooler vertically on AM4, here's a quick little pro tip that will save you a big headache to. The problem is almost every single cooler out there, including the stock cooler, uses the built in backplate that comes with the motherboard. But when you remove the factory retention mechanism that holds it in place to go and install the cooler itself, it always falls off because there's nothing really holding it in place besides the pressure when you actually have something mounted, and trust me, it can get really annoying. So what I do is I just take a little piece of electrical tape and run it along the back plate and secure it to the motherboard temporarily. This way I can actually put some force on the mount to get the first two screws started, which then will at least hold the back plate in place. So we can go ahead and remove the electrical tape. Which, trust me, is a lot easier than trying to hold the back of the PC case and screw the cooler in at the same time. Yeah, that's never fun. But if you wanna avoid this issue all together, install your air cooler while you're building the PC outside of the case. But if you have to do it while it's inside, the electrical tape is your best friend.
