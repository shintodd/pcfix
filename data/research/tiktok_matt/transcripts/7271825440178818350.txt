This is the Intel Core 2 extreme X 6800, codename Conro XL, and it was the best CPU for gaming back in 2006. The CPU featured 2 cores and 2 threads, but these cores are running out of stock speed. That was a staggering 2.933 gigahertz, which is really what set it apart from the rest of the competition at not. And to make things even better, as implied by the extreme name, the CPU was fully unlocked, allowing for even more performance if you were to overclock it. Speaking of the extreme name, this was also the first time that Intel introduced Extreme Edition processors into the market. With that said, to get this incredibly fast CPU back in 2006, this chip would have set you back over 1000 dollars.
