can you install a GPU cooler on a CPU? well, let's find out. in order to try this, I took a GT740 from EVGA due to its small form factor to remove the cooler from the car. then I took the removed cooler, plugged in the fan connector to the motherboard's fan header, and placed the cooler on top of the CPU with some thermal paste. once that was complete, we booted up the system and ended up working just fine with idle temperatures sitting in the mid 30s. so it turns out you can install a GPU cooler on a CPU.
