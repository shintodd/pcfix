A customer just brought in this 20 year old computer and they said they need some old photos off of it as the system is no longer booting. So in order to do this, I took the drive out of the system by removing the mounting hardware and then sliding the drive right out in the front of the case. With that drive now on this system, I can then use this Star Tech IDE, the USB adapter, to connect the drive to any modern computer. So with that now connected to a PC, I can now locate and drag and drop any necessary files over to a flash drive for the customer so they can access it from any device. With that data now backed up, we can now get this flash drive with the pictures back to the customer.
