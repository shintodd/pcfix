Cyber Power PC? More like cyber problems PC, because this gaming PC has been shutting off randomly on my customer anytime that it's under load. Well this is usually a pretty sure sign of a bad cooling system. Well when I ran a stress test on the system and using 80 64, the system overheated nearly instantly, crashing in the process as soon as it reached a little over 100 degrees Celsius. So the first thing I wanna do in these types of circumstance is always to check the CPU cooler Thermal paste. As usually if it's overheating this fast, there's no broken fans or anything like that, it's probably the paste. And after pulling off the cooler when you know it, this Thermal paste was completely dried up to the point that it basically looked like it had evaporated. See what happens with Thermal paste? Over time the compound starts to degrade and this can lead to an increased rate of dry out. Which is why it's really important to change your Thermal paste out every couple years. So to fix this computer, all we gotta do is clean off the dried up Thermal paste and then take some Arctic MX six Thermal paste and put that onto the CPU and reinstall the cooler. Just like that, this system now runs in the 40s under a full load stress test instead of instantly overheating before. So we can now get this old but running nice and cool gaming PC Back to our customer
