This is an Nvidia GTX 07:50 Ti released back in 2014. This was the ultimate budget graphics card for gaming. What made this GPU such a popular option for budget gamers was the fact that the card at the time could run most games at 10 ADPU at no problem. And because most models were so power efficient, they drew less than 60 Watts, meaning they could actually run off just the Pcie power and didn't need any additional power cables, making them perfect to turn any off the shelf PC that you could buy at any store and into a fully capable gaming system. And not to mention these cards only sold for $150.
