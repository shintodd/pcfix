have you ever wondered what would happen if you try to install RAM while your system was running? well, I did too. so I decided to try it out. to do this, I went ahead and grabbed a 16GB kit of RAM, 2 8GB sticks, and I left one stick running in the system and had windows open with Task Manager while this is running. I decided to go ahead and plug in the other stick of RAM. and immediately, to my surprise, the system completely shut off. I thought the system might have been fried. however, the system quickly rebooted into my surprise, it came back into Windows quickly and when it did, I was able to check in Task Manager if both sticks of RAM were showing up. and to my surprise, they were, so it didn't end up frying the ram or the motherboard, luckily. but I really wouldn't recommend this at home. if you guys do want to see more content like this, make sure you drop a like and share this with a friend who may find this interesting.
