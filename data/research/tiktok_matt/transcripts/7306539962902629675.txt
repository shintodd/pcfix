A viewer asked what would happen if you combined a Thermal pad with Thermal paste. Well, let's go ahead and find out. To do this, we place the small dot of Thermal paste on our CPU and place the Thermal pad on top. Surprisingly, our results found that under a full load, the combination of the two actually works slightly better than the Thermal pad by itself, with max temperatures only reaching 68 degrees Celsius compared to 72 degrees with just a single Thermal pad. This was very interesting to see, and I assume it was related to the Thermal paste acting as a way to fill in the air gaps between the Thermal pad and the CPUs heat spreader.
