have you ever had a device where you found the battery that becomes swollen and wonder what actually causes this to even happen? well, this is due to how lithium ion batteries actually work, as these batteries operate off of a chemical reaction, which over time starts to become less efficient, producing a gas byproduct, which because these batteries are enclosed, that gas has nowhere to escape, thus causing it to swell. if you ever notice this happening to one of your devices, you want to get that battery replaced as soon as possible, as the swelling, if it gets too bad, can start to damage other components inside of your device or even cause personal injury or even a fire to start if that battery pack ever becomes punctured.
