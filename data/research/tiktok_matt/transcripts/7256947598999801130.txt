This Dell XPS is a crazy overheating issue as whenever you fire up any task, even the web browser, the system will instantly peak at 100 degrees Celsius and the system will finally calm down to about 60 degrees or even higher, which is really too hot at idle. So to get this system fixed, we're gonna go ahead and replace the CPU and GPU. To do this, I first unscrewed and removed the bottom cover off the laptop and then unplug the battery to be safe and unscrewed the heatsink. After doing so, I noticed that there was actually a decent amount of dry out of the old Thermal paste. So after getting the heat sink, CPU and GPU dye cleaned off, I put some Arctic MX 6 on and after reassembling the laptop, we instantly saw a 10 degree drop at idle and the system was far more reliable when it came to max temperatures when operating any kind of various tasks like web browsing. With the system no longer a burning hazard, we can now get the system back to the customer.
