If you ever have a laptop that's not charging and you can't get the system of power on no matter what you do, well, the first thing I always recommend is what's called e power reset. See, this laptop just came into my shop as our customers said that one morning their system just stopped charging and no longer would turn on even after the charger was even changed. And even though this issue definitely sounds like a bad battery, I always like to start with a power reset, which is where you first remove the bottom cover off your laptop and then actually disconnect the battery from the motherboard. Then press and hold the power button for about 10 seconds and then reconnect the battery and plug everything back in. I usually do this while the system was on its back so I don't have to close up and reopen the system if it doesn't happen to work. But in the case of this laptop, it successfully got the system back up and running. See, what this power reset does is that it resets the CMOS. Very similar to pulling out the CR 2032 coin battery out on your desktop. Resetting any kind of invalid settings or airs on startup. In order to verify nothing else was wrong or anything could have been causing this to begin with, I ran the built in diagnostic utility from HP and everything checked out in the system. So with the system now Good to go. We can go and close it up and get it back over to our customer.
