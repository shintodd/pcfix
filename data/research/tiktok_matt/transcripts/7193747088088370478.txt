this AMD Radeon HD 7970 was released in 2011 and this AMD R9280X was released in 2013. although they have different names and were launched nearly 2 years apart from one another, these GPUs are nearly identical. this is because the R9280X was just a factory overclocked version of the exact same silicone that came with the 7970. this meaning that if you bought a 7970 and got lucky with a decent overclock, you might just have the exact same performance that if you were to buy a 280x 2 years later.
