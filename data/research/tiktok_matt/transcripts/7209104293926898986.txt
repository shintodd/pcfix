Customer just brought in this gaming PC that they were building. They said it wouldn't boot, so let's evaluate it and get it fixed. Well, right off the bat, I noticed a few issues that I can tell that would cause the system not to boot. First off, we were missing the front panel connectors that weren't installed. And most importantly, we were missing the power EPS connector. That's for the CPU. So after rewiring the entire system and plugging in all the cables properly, we were able to get the system to boot up just fine. So all we have to do now is put the rest of the case components back together, and we can now get the system back to the customer.
