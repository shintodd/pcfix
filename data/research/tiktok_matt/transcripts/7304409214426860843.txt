I just bought a 1 pound block of copper. Do you think it can cool a CPU? Well, let's find out. After putting some Thermal paste on the CPU and placing the block on top, the system actually booted up. Additionally, while the system was sitting at idle, the copper block was doing just fine, or as least as good as the stock cooler was doing before. But let's test under a full load. After testing under a full load, the system lasted for about a minute and 30 seconds before it started overheating and quickly hit 100 degrees Celsius. So the answer is yes, under a limited load it can cool it, but not under any considerable load.
