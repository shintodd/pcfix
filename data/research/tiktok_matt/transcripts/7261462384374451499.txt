This gaming PC doesn't have any display out after the customer decided to go ahead and upgrade their GPU and RAM, so let's get it fixed with them. And after just properly seating the memory modules, the system fired right up without an issue. Also, while we have the system here, a good rule of thumb that you should follow is that if you have to use a splitter for your Pcie power cables, your power supply is likely not powerful enough for your GPU. Or it's probably something that you likely need to upgrade because it's probably a very cheap unit if it doesn't have 28 pin connectors.
