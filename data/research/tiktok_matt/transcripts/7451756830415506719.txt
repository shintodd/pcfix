You won't believe what caused this leanly fan right here to power on but get no RGB lighting. Because after pulling out the three fans, when I actually started pulling them apart, I found a tiny bit of Thermal paste on one of the pogo pins, which actually prevented the full connection required for the RGB signal to be sent between the two fans as it's passing through through the daisy chain interface. So after just simply wiping that off, the RGB came right back, which was just kind of crazy to see. That Thermal paste can absolutely get everywhere if you're not careful with it.
