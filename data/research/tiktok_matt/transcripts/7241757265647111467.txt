I was just taking apart this all in one PC to fix some overheating issues and when I went to go ahead and remove the heat sink off the CPU, the Thermal paste was completely dried up. This is a perfect example why you should repast your CPU every few years to prevent dry out like this as it can cause overheating like in this case as the Thermal paste is not able to actually fill in the gaps as it's supposed to between the heat sink and the CPU.
