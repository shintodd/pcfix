A simple PC upgrading mistake made this a Noble Legion gaming PC feel like it was nearly 10 years old. See, the customer of this machine just brought it in because they said that their system became unbearably slow after upgrading their drive. And after verifying this for myself, the system takes about two to three minutes to boot and almost a minute just to launch a simple web browser. Which doesn't make a whole lot of sense for a desktop that's equipped with the M dot 2 N V M E S S D. But this started to make a whole lot more sense the second I noticed that the M dot 2 drive inside the system was still factory, meaning that the drive that they upgraded wasn't the S S D. Rather than mechanical hard drive. And as a cautionary measure, I went ahead and open up File Explorer on the PC. Guess what? That was the only drive that was showing up, despite us knowing that the system should have two different drives. And after a quick examination of the properties of this drive, the customer was running Windows on the new 2 terabyte mechanical hard drive in the 256 gigabyte internal SSD which is sitting there waiting to be formatted. So what this means is our customer made a simple and common mistake by installing Windows on their mechanical hard drive rather than their SSD to fix an issue like this. It's quite simple. We're just Gonna have to reinstall Windows using our USB installation media onto the SSD this time. Set our 2 terabyte mechanical drive as the secondary data drive. And after completing that, the system is now back up and running and ready for some games. So let's go and get it back to our customer.
