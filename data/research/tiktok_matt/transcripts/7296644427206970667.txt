When I had opened up one of my favorite hard drive checking tools which is called Crystal Discompo, which showed, as I expected, that the drive was operating in a caution state due to some uncorrectable sector. Anytime you have a drive in a caution state like this, you immediately wanna get the driver place to prevent any data loss or downtime. In order to do this, after speaking with my customer, we decided to go and replace the internal drive with SSD from crucial. To go ahead and get this installed, we first have to undo the bottom two screws which pops off the back cover and then that go ahead and be removed. From there we can then unscrew one screw that allows us to go inside out the hard drive. And after doing so we can see how disgusting this PC is, full of hair and dust. So let's get this cleaned up a little bit. With the system now cleaned up because this is an older system, it Wasn't designed for SSDs so I used a two and a half to three and a half inch adapter to go ahead and get a perfect fit for the installation. After getting the system all put back together the health of the hard drive wasn't safe enough to go ahead and clone all the data over directly.
