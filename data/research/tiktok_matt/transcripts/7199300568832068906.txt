Customer just brought in this laptop that was running slow, so let's upgrade into an SSD to increase performance. In order to do this, we first need to remove the back cover off the laptop that's secured by 13 Phillips head screws. This then reveals the internal components of the laptop, where the battery and old hard drive then can be removed. With the old drive out, we can take this Sandisk SSD and move over the old mounting hardware from the old drive and then get the SSD installed in the system. With that now installed, we can reassemble the system and fire it up. After performing a clean installation of windows on the system as requested by the customer, the laptop is good to go and running faster than ever. So we can now get the system back to the customer.
