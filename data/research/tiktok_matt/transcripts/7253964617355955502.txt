I don't think I've ever seen as much dust come out of the laptop as this MSI workstation, as after using some compressed air, 2 dust clouds came right out of the laptop. And the downside of letting a laptop get like this is that because these fans blow through a heat sink to keep your CPU and GPU cool, once they get clogged with dust, it hurts the amount of airflow that can penetrate through, thus reducing the overall cooling performance of the fans. So I highly recommend every once in a while to clean out your laptops to prevent dust build up like this from happening.
