This old HP laptop has a destroyed screen as apparently it got punched somehow. And I normally wouldn't recommend replacing a screen on such an old system, but my customer insisted, so let's get it swapped out. To do this, we first need to remove the battery on the bottom of the laptop. And then from there, on this model, we just have to pull up and unscrew the two bottom corner screws at the bottom of the hinges, which then allows us to carefully remove the bezel around the screen. Once that's out of the way, we then can unscrew the six screws at the bottom of the screen, followed by two up top, which will actually release the back cover. And now we just have to unscrew two more screws on either side, which basically attaches the hinge to the side of the actual screen itself. And once that's removed, the screen can now be detached and we can get it unplugged and swapped out with our brand new screen. And we can start to reassemble the system the exact same way it was taken apart. First attaching the screen to the hinge and then bringing up the back cover and mounting that back with the eight screws that we initially removed, and finally reinstalling the bezel, followed by the battery. After a quick test, the system is now good to go with a functional screen and we can get the system back to our customer.
