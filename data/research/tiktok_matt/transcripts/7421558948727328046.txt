Here are three ways the brand new Aurora R16 is terrible for repair and upgrades. First off, look at this motherboard. What kind of form factor is this? Not anything standard, so good luck trying to get a replacement when this one eventually fails. Speaking of proprietary hardware, look at this power supply. It's also a non standard size. And because Dell thought that 500 watt power supply would be perfect for a system like this, when you eventually want to upgrade your GPU, well, you're out of luck getting an upgraded one. Lastly, what probably bothers me more than anything is the fact that Alienware thought it would be a good idea to go ahead and ditch the standard and use their own power connectors. Just so consumers can never diagnose their system with a spare power supply. Or replace it with one that's not specially made for the system.
