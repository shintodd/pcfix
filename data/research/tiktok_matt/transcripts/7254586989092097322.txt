A customer just brought in this HP laptop as the system wouldn't turn on, so let's get it fixed. After doing a quick evaluation and confirming that the system wouldn't turn on, I noticed that the system's charging LED indicator was actually off and the charging cable wasn't going in all the way. So I looked at the charge port to find that the plastic around the charge port had broken off, causing a weak connection between the DC Jack and the power cable. So in order to fix this, I removed the bottom cover of the laptop and then disconnected the battery from there. I then unscrewed the 3 screws holding down the right hinge and then removed the old charge port where I was able to confirm the damage. From there I took this brand new port, plug that in, and then put the system back together. And when I went to go ahead and fire the system back up, it loaded right into Windows without an issue. So it looks like we just had a broken charge port in this case and we can now get this repaired laptop back to the customer.
