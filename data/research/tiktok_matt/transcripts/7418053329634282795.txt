This hot box of a gaming PC is reaching over 106 degrees Celsius on the CPU, causing the system to frequently freeze and has pretty much caused it to become unusable. What's the blame for this high heat all of a sudden? Well this case has only one front air intake and it's suffocated by dust as the build up around the fan is so bad this 200 millimeter high airflow fan is it's barely moving any air through the system. That, along with the fact that there's no exhaust for this case creates this hot box environment where the heat has to passively dissipate through the mesh panels, which really isn't ideal. So to going to fix this overheating issue? I started by cleaning out the dust out of the front air intake and then when I went to go ahead and pull off the CPU cooler I noticed that it wasn't fully secure, which caused the stock Thermal paste to not fully make contact with the actual CPU and cooler, eventually causing it to dry up. So no wonder the system was so hot there was no way for the heat to transfer from the actual CPU to the cooler itself. You're cleaning up the dried up Thermal paste and repasting the CPU along with adding the exhaust fan to the actual case. Took our 106 degrees CPU from crashing to sitting in the 70s under a stress test, taking our PC from being completely unusable back to Running better than it was when it was probably first built.
