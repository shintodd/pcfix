You break, I fix. More like you break and I'll make it worse. Because when you take your PC to a repair shop, you expect a 400 dollar repair to actually work or at least not present any new issues. But our customer, after taking their system to the local ubreakifix shop, was told that their power supply need to be upgraded to go and prevent their PC from randomly shutting off. But when they brought their system home, they found that they were getting no display out. Which is why it's here at my shop now. And after a quick inspection, I immediately knew that we were dealing with insufficient power to the GPU. This Asus card has a little red LED that will only light up if the card isn't receiving power properly, and I confirmed this by using my Doctor power 3 power supply tester where it showed it was only reading 150 Watts from that 12 volt high power connector instead of the rated 450 that it should be received. Why would a brand new 1,000 watt power supply provide insufficient power to a GPU? Well it turns out the ubreakifix technician changed every cable out except for the Pcie cables for the splitter for the brand new power supply. And how I figured that this was going on is that these were the only two cables that weren't sleeved the same way that Corsair typically manufactures their cable. And this costly mistake ended up frying The 12 volt high power connector, as I confirmed this with a secondary power supply because that was also not reading out properly. To fix this issue, I ran a brand new native 12 volt high power connector from Corsair directly from the power supply right over to the GPU and the system fired right up. Or at least most of it. I couldn't figure out why the NZXT cooler wasn't lighting up, but then I realized that the Seida power that it was being used for, that was actually zip tied unlike all the other cables. Meaning it was likely from the old power supply installed by the customer originally. So after swapping that out, the cooler fired right up and this PC is now running perfectly. So let's go and get it back over to our customer!
