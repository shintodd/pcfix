When someone brings in a PC with what they believe is a bad GPU giving them no display out, you expect the system to have the same issue when you plug it in. But in the case of this NZXT pre built, it works perfectly fine. There were no problems whatsoever when I hooked it up to my monitor, and it was even completely stable under a stress test when I ran Furmark. Well then why did my customers say they weren't getting any display? Well this issue seems to have all started when they actually swapped out their display cable to their monitor. And when I asked him what port they plugged into on their GPU, they said the port on their motherboard when I showed them a picture. Which those normally do work unless you have an F series processor. Likely in the case of this system that actually disables those from working because they don't feature integrated graphics. Plus, ideally anytime you have a GPU in your system, it's always the best practice. Make sure that you actually plug into the horizontal ports located on the card itself and not the motherboard ports. It'll save you plenty of headaches. If you ever have an issue like this, make sure you're always plugging into your GPU. This result in one of the easiest repairs were I probably just call it a fix in quite a while, but I'm glad the customer can now get back to gaming and They don't have to shell out a bunch of money for a brand new card. So let's go and get this PC back over to them.
