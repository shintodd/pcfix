Here's how I was able to drop this GPU's operating temperature and increase performance in just a couple minutes. As this GPU was about 8 years old, it was time for a repasting. So I took the GPU apart, cleaned off the old Thermal paste, reapplied some new Thermal paste, and put the card back together. Once that was done, we saw an immediate effect with the GPU running about 4 degrees Celsius cooler under our full load stress test, and we even saw a slight boost to our 3D Mark times by scores. So if you have an older GPU, it may be time to replace it to get it back to peak performance.
