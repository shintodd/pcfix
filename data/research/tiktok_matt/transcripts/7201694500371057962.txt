This is a 100 custom water cooling kit that I purchased on Amazon and actually performs quite well. This includes nearly everything you need to water cool your CPU and GPU after installing the system and filling up the loop. I was surprised to see some pretty solid results, as my 8700K and R9280X I chose for this build were running at 32 and 28 degrees Celsius respectively. At idle and even under a full system stress test, the CPU is sitting around 77 degrees Celsius and the GPU was sitting at 47 degrees Celsius. The system did perform way better than I honestly could have expected for less than 100, but I did have a couple issues with the system. First off, let's start with the pump. It honestly was just way too loud. And the overall quality of the other parts in the system were also just really cheaply made, including the radiator fittings, the water block, and the tubing that were included. Which does concern me for some long term deployment issues. With that said, I do think if you're looking to get in water cooling for your first time and you just want to try it out, this could be a great starter kit as you can slowly upgrade the various aspects of the loop as needed.
