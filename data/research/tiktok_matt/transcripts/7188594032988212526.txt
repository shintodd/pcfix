can you cool a CPU with just a fan and no heat sink? well, let's find out. in order to test this, I took the most powerful fan I had, which was this 10 000 RPM fan, and stuck it directly over my CPU with some helping hands. once that was complete, I fired up the system and at idle the temperatures weren't all that bad as they were sitting around 50 degrees Celsius. in order to test this further, I ran a CPU stress test where it wasn't even able to last 25 seconds before the CPU started to overheat. so it looks like you can't cool a CPU without a heat sink, even with a 10 000 RPM fan.
