Because when you go to turn the system on, all the lights and fans fire right up, which we can see right here, but we end up with no display out every single time. So what exactly is going on here? Well, anytime you have a PC that turns on and fires up just fine, but you end up with no display out. Well, that's a perfect example of a system failing the POST. Otherwise known as the power on self test that your motherboard runs on every single startup to check the health and compatibility of all your internal components like your CPU, RAM, GPU, and storage devices to ensure you have a smooth user experience operation. However, as this test does take place before your system actually gets a display out, if one of the components happens to fail the test, well, the system won't display anything out, letting you know something's up, just like what's been happening here. So then what of those components is causing the system to obviously not post? Well, anytime I have a system that's failed to post like this, the first thing I'm looking for is anything loose or any kind of LEDs on the motherboard or case. I might be giving away some kind of clue. However, in this case, we don't have any visual clues, but we do have an auditory one. As the second I turn the system on, I Noticed a faint beeping coming from inside the system that doesn't appear to be coming from the motherboard side, but rather around back from this top hard drive right here. And a continuous beep coming from a hard drive like that usually means the drives in failure. Which wouldn't surprise me with this being 20 years old. So I bet we simply disconnect this drive from data and power, then go to fire this PC now back up. Or now wouldn't you look at that? It fires right up to Windows. our failure to post was all related back to this bad hard drive. Which I ended up proving by hooking it up again where we were back to no display.
