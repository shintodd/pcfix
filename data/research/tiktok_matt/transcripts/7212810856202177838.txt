A customer just brought in this Dell laptop saying that the battery was dying way faster than ever before. So let's get it fixed. First, to verify the issue, I ran dell's built in diagnostic tool, which found that the battery was at its end of life and rated it in a fair condition. So let's get it replaced. In order to do this, I first had to remove the rear cover off the laptop, and after that was done, the battery then could be unscrewed, unplugged and removed. From there a new battery can be installed and the system can be reassembled. With the system now back together, all that has to be done is a battery calibration and we can get the system back to the customer.
