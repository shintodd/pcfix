I honestly can't get over how easy MSI made it to install a brand new SSD into one of their new motherboards. All you have to do is push this little latch in that will allow you to pull off the actual heat sink for your SSD. And then if you take your drive and slot it into place, all you have to do is just push it down and it locks into place. Thanks to their new Easy Mount system as the mount is actually spring loaded, making it so easy for installation in removals. Just as easy as all you have to do is actually just slide that over to the side and the drive pops right out. I think that easy DIY approach that MSI is taking needs to be followed across the entire industry because.
