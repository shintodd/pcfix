here's how I was able to turn on my PC with this toggle switch. first you need 6 things, a way to cut and strip wire, a screwdriver, a PC power switch, 2 pin cable, a momentary toggle switch, a cover, and lastly a plate or location. you plan to mount the hardware to start first cut the power switch cable as close as you can. to the power button. then strip the wires about a back. next, loosen the screws on the toggle switch itself and wrap the stripped wire around them and tighten it back down. next loosen the collar around the switch and place the switch in the mounting plate. then place the cover over the switch. then place the collar back on. lastly, plug in your 2 pin header into the front panel connectors on your motherboard and then you can turn your PC on with the toggle switch.
