So from there I wanted to see if maybe we were dealing with a power issue. So I then plugged in one of my known good power supplies into the system, but that didn't make a difference either. At that point there was no power seeing the move through the motherboard, so I thought I would go ahead and re seat the CPU just in case that was the issue. After removing it, I found the issue. There were 6 to 7 bent pins inside of the socket. So let's get that motherboard swapped out. And after rewiring the system and putting everything back together, we did a quick boot up and the system fired right up into the bio. So it looks like the only issue was a damaged socket.
