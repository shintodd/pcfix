There's one feature that everybody should have enabled on their computer, and you can access it by clicking the Windows icon, typing in the word restore, and clicking the option create a restore point. This will bring up the System Protection menu, and in here you want to make sure that next to your local disc it says Protection is on. If it's not, click configure and turn on System Protection. What exactly does this do? Well, it turns on System Restore, so anytime that you have an update or install a new app, it actually creates a restore point on your computer that you can go back to if something's wrong. And if you don't have one set up? If you ever end up at a blue screen after installing a bad program or update, you might end up at the point where you just can't do a system restore because you don't have a restore point to go back to, causing you to possibly lose files and your settings. After working in IT repair for almost 10 years now, I can't tell you how many systems I've saved by being able to use that feature to go back to when somebody thought they lost absolutely everything. It's not an excuse to not have a backup drive also, but it is very helpful if you accidentally install a bad update or bad program.
