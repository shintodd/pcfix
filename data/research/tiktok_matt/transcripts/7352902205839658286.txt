It feels like over the years GPS have gotten absolutely ridiculous when it comes to their size, price, and of course power efficiency. But all that changes today as Nvidia just dropped their brand new RTX 40 80 Mini, which thanks to some brand new manufacturing processes, they are able to shrink the full RTX 40 80 GPU dye into the form factor of a USB C flash drive. You may ask, how does this actually work? Well this isn't a typical USB C port, rather a Thunderbolt port which allows for the full transmission signal of the same PCI interface. So you can expect the exact same performance out of this as a brand new full size RTX 40 80, but with the benefit of it being a fraction of the price, size, and power draw. And it's honestly so efficient that in fact the fans never even have to turn on. Plus the best part of the RTX 40 80 Mini is that it's portable and compatible with a ton of devices, including Macbooks. You can finally truly game on any device, even a Mac. And as of right now, the RTX 40 80 Mini is on sale for only $100, which is game changing if you ask me.
