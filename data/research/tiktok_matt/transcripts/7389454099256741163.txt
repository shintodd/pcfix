Sometimes we get lucky with the computers that come into our shop. See, this Dell laptop was just dropped off because the owner claimed that the disc would sometimes skip, and they believe that their DVD drive was at fault. An old issue, for sure, that most people really don't have to deal with these days. But when I went to actually pull out the CD to see what was going on that they had inside their laptop, I noticed that the entire disc was scratched. And it wasn't even like some minor scratches. Looks like somebody took the disc, went outside, and scraped it on some concrete, because there were. And just scratches all over. So no wonder this disc wouldn't play, right? And to confirm this even further, I threw in a DVD that they also brought with them into the system, and guess what? That played just fine. So looks like this might have just been one of the simplest repairs that I've ever made. And with the system now good to go, let's go and get it back over to our customer.
