This might have been AMD best GPU they've ever made from a value perspective. This is the AMD RX four eighty released back in 2016. This card was a 1080 p beast at the time, and it completely undercut both AMD own graphics card lineup and Nvidia by launching this card at just under $200. This is an amazing deal compared to the competition. However, most people were actually never able to buy these. This is because these were basically the face of crypto mining early on and were constantly out of stock due to their incredibly cheap price tag and efficient hash rate.
