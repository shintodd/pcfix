Can you cool a CPU using a cup of ice? Well, in order to find out, I took a copper cup, wrapped it in a towel to absorb any extra moisture, and stuck in on my CPU with some Thermal paint. Once it was complete, island the ice cube and fired up the system in the windows. After adding a couple extra ice cubes to the cup, the CPU is idling around 8 to 10 degrees Celsius. So in order to really put this to the test, I fired up a CPU stress test where it ran for over 10 minutes as I slowly added more ice as needed. By doing this, the CPU ended up settling around 48 degrees Celsius. So it turns out you can't cool CPU with a cup of ice. What should we try next to cool CPU? Let me know in the comments down below.
