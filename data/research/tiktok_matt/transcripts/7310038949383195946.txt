This HB laptop just came into my shop as my customer was saying that they have really poor battery life, so let's get this fixed. To do this, I first wanted to go ahead and check the health of the battery by first opening up command prompt and running the command power config battery report. Once open, we can see at the system's battery was only operating at less than 65% of its usable capacity. So to get this battery swapped out, at first unscrewed and remove the bottom cover off the laptop, and then from there we can unplug, unscrew and remove the old battery and swap in our brand new battery by getting that screwed in the place and then plug back in. After reassembling the system, we can now fire it up. But instead of loading right into Windows, I wanted to go ahead and first press escape, followed by selecting System Diagnostics, we can go over to the component test and select the battery as this will allow us to run a battery calibration so that before we even get into Windows, Windows as well as the rest of the system hardware actually will recognize the new batteries full capacity. So after letting that run for a few hours, this HP laptop is now good to go and we can get it back to our customer.
