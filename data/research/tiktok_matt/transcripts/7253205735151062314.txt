I've been asked number of times why I started making TikToks for my business and honestly there are 2 main reasons why I started, why I continue to make daily content. First off, from a business perspective, TikTok has allowed me to change my customer demographic from just being within a 15 mile radius to now being able to service people's computers from across the globe. And on top of that, TikTok has allowed me to fulfill my mission as a business owner, which has been to help others and support and repair their computers. No matter if I'm doing the work or if it's the customer doing it themselves, I'm just happy to keep more computers out of the landfill.
