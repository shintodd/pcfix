This little CR 2032 battery right here that looks like it belongs in a watch is what's called a CMOS battery. And it's got a very simple job to provide just a little bit of power to the motherboard when you happen to lose power from the wall. This way your motherboard can remember two important things. First, your real time clock, which is in charge of your date and time information, which is why this motherboard still thinks it's 2019. And to keep power to your computer's BIOS chip, which remembers things like your boot order, memory overclocking profiles, and any tweaks you made in your BIOS.
