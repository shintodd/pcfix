This is the Nvidia GTX 2 95, the fastest GPU for gaming in 2009. What made this GPU so special outside of its performance was the fact that it wasn't one GPU. Rather, it was two GPUs and one actually working together. But instead of both chips being on the same PCB like other dual GPU cards at the time, video decided to make two different PCBs with separate GPUs. So you can almost think of it like a two way SLI setup in a single dual solid card. The GPUs together ran at 576 megahertz and has a total of 1,700 ninety two megabytes in memory.
