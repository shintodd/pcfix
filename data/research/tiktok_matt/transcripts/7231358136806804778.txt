Customer just brought in this Dell laptop saying that the battery was only lasting 20 minutes. So let's get a fix for them. In order to first check the health of the battery, I first fired up the built in Dell diagnostic tool which saw that the battery was at its end of life and was only operating at 40% of its usable. So in order to get this fixed, I first removed the bottom cover off the laptop where the battery then could be unscrewed, unplugged and removed. From there I then installed this brand new battery and put the system back together. And at that point I fired the system back up into the diagnostic tool. We can see that the battery is now being properly recognized and with this system now fixed, we can now get it back to the customer.
