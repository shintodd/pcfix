If you ever see a CPU fan air on startup but your fans are still running, like in the case of this gaming PC, it likely means you didn't plug your CPU cooler into the proper CPU fan header on your motherboard. As that was the case with this gaming PC, as a cooler was plugged into one of the system fan headers rather than the actual CPU header, and once plugged into the proper location, the air went away. However, for some reason you can't plug into the CPU fan header. One way you can go around this is to enter your BIOS and navigate, usually under the monitoring tab, which is located in the advanced mode of most BIOS. And then from there if you look for the CPU Fan speed monitoring tab, you can then usually select a drop down to go ahead and select the ignore option. Which will basically trick your system into thinking that it doesn't need to actually check for a CPU fan if you don't happen to have one plugged into that main header.
