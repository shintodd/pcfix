A customer just brought in this Dell laptop and the screen is pretty messed up with parts of it being completely defective. So let's get this swapped out. In order to do this, I first loosened the 8 captive screws on the bottom cover and then removed it from the bottom of the laptop. The first thing you always want to do anytime you're working with any kind of laptop that has integrated batteries to disconnect the battery from the motherboard. This will prevent you from accidentally turning the system on while you're working on it or possibly causing some kind of short. From there, I then started disconnecting all the cables associated with the display assembly, including the Wi Fi display, proximity sensor, and microphone and webcam cables. Once this were all removed, I unscrewed the 2 hinges and then lifted the bottom half of the laptop up, making it able to be separated from the display assembly itself. Now all I have to do is take this brand new display assembly and attach it the exact same way that I just took the other one off, and then lower down the hinges and screw them in. Following that, I can reconnect all of the cables inside of the laptop, including all of the sensors as well as the battery last. With that all complete, the bottom cover can now be reinstalled and after testing out the screen, we can see that it is good as new and with This now fixed. We can now get the system back to the customer.
