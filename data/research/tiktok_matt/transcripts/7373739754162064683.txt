I've been waiting forever for this repair tool. This is Doctor Power 3 from thermaltech and it's a successor to Doctor Power 2, which is a power supply tester that lets you quickly verify the health of your power supply. And what's so great about this new model is that because it's ATX 3.1 ready, it can now test 12 + 4 pin Pcie connectors and even tell you data like if the cables rated for 300 Watts, 400 Watts, or 600 Watts. Which is super cool and I can't wait to continue my testing with this tool. And you'll see plenty of repairs with it in the future!
