And a flash drive that has a Cronus True Image installed on it that allows us to go ahead and do the cloning process. Once both those are plugged into the system, we can go ahead and load into a Cronus and go ahead and select the old drive as our source disk and set the new SSD that the customer installed as the destination. From there we can go ahead and let the system run. And after a couple hours the clone is now complete in the operating system and all the customers files have been directly moved over.
