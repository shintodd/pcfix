There's one issue with laptops that I think needs to be addressed by all manufacturers, and that's the way that screws are mounted inside the laptops. As in the case of this Toshiba laptop, the hinge is really loose, and if we actually open it up, we can see that the metal inserts that are used to actually hold the screw in place broke off under the hinge. This is because the metal inserts that are actually holding the screw in place are actually pressed into the case mold at the factory, and this sits in plastic, which actually deteriorates over time and eventually breaks off like this. I really would like to see laptop manufacturers step up and try to make this part of the laptop a little more rigid to prevent unnecessary brakes like this.
