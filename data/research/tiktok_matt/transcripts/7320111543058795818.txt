This pre built gaming PC runs incredibly hot as an idle. The system sits around 60 degrees Celsius, and the second you throw it under any kind of load it instantly spikes to 100 degrees Celsius and starts the Thermal throttle. So let's go ahead and get the system fixed. See, the second I turned on the system actually a pretty solid idea of what was going on as I noticed that the AIO's pump lock was actually making some weird noises. Kind of like a grinding noise, which is almost a sure indication of a failing or bad pump. So let's go and swap this AIO out with one. From there. Do this, we first need to go ahead and remove the pump block off the CPU, and then from there we can go and unscrew it and remove the radiator and fans from up top. With old cooler out of the way, we can now go ahead and mount the radiator up top and then attach our pump block with the pre applied Thermal paste onto the CPU. And then attach the LCD screen cover onto the pump that now installed. I went ahead and tested the new cooler out to see that idle temperatures are now sitting in the mid 20s, and even under a full load stress test I didn't see the system go above the mid 60s.
