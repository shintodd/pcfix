Have you ever wondered what would happen if you try to install RAM while your system was running? Well I did too, so I decided to try it out. To do this I went ahead and grabbed a 16 gigabyte kit of RAM, two 8 gigabyte sticks, and I left one stick running in the system and had Windows open with Task Manager. While this is running I decided to go ahead and plug in the other sticker RAM and immediately to my surprise, the system completely shut off. I thought the system might have been fried, however the system quickly rebooted and to my surprise it came back into Windows quickly. And when it did, I was able to check in Task Manager if both sticks of RAM were showing up, and to my surprise they were. So it didn't end up frying the RAM or the motherboard, luckily.
