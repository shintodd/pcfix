The SARX 4 74 massive X came with one of the coolest features. It was called Hard Swap Fans and allowed users to quickly swap out their GPU fans just by pinching a release mechanism and lifting up. This feature made it significantly easier to clean and service your GPU if a fan ever died. It also allowed users to swap out their fans between the stock black fans and ones with LEDs like these. I personally would like to see this feature be brought back. What do you guys think?
