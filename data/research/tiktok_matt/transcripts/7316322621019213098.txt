I'm always asked Matt, what Matt are you working on? Well, this is the mod Matt extreme from mod right, and it's an anti static work surface with some really cool and useful graphics for when working on Pcs, including wiring diagrams, socket layout, phantom radiator sizes, along for guides for when you're doing water cooling. I've had this one for about seven years now and it's held up great despite repairing thousands of computers on it, spilling Thermal paste, dropping dry ice on it, and all kinds of other stuff. So if you're looking for a nice work surface to work on PCs, I highly recommend this one.
