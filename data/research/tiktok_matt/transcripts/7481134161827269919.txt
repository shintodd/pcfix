A quick little PC building pro tip for anybody building a system, especially for somebody else, is to take out the GPU IO covers and instead of throwing these away, go and put them in the ports on your motherboard for any display outs. Cause I can't tell you as a computer technician how many times I've gotten a call where somebody tells me I just moved and all of a sudden I went ahead and plugged my computer back in. Now I'm getting no display out for me to go and ask them well what ports are you plugged into? They send me a picture and they're always plugged into the motherboards IO. And although a lot of CPUs have integrated graphics that do allow for these ports to work, there's a lot on the market that don't like these F series CPUs. And you shouldn't be using this port anyways unless you're doing it for like a testing purpose. Because if you're paying for something like a GPU, you might as well use it. And when you try to run off these integrated ports, most of the time this is just going to use those integrated graphics on the CPU, which are going to be much slower. And even if you're building the PC for yourself, it's still a good idea to actually cover these up just so you don't forget one day and accidentally plug into that port to either get no display out or just poor Performance
