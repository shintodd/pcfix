A customer just brought in their Dell laptop saying that it wouldn't charge. So to verify the issue, we fired up the system and ran those built in diagnostic tool where we can see that even with the battery drain, the system showed up as a charger at an idle status, meaning it wasn't charging, and the AC adapter showed up as unknown. First thing we wanted to try in this case was to swap out the charger itself, and after doing so, the system immediately started charging without an issue. So it looks like in this case, we're just dealing with a bad charger, and we can get the laptop and a new charger back to the customer.
