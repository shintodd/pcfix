This is the swap fans from Thermaltake and what makes it so unique is the fact that you can swap out the fan blades for ones that reverse the direction of airflow, meaning it gives you more customizability when building your PC as you no longer have to look at the ugly side of the fan as you once did. The reverse direction of airflow. Thermaltake actually made this process very easy to do as well. All you have to do is flip the fan over and use your thumbs to push the old fan blade out, and then take the included reversible blade and push it down to secure it. It's that simple and I'm personally a huge fan of this innovation. I hope to see more companies adopt similar concepts to this in the future.
