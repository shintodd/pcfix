a customer just brought in this gaming PC that they just built and they said it wasn't turning on. so let's get it fixed. after doing a quick inspection of the wiring inside the system, I noticed that the customer was using an 8 pin CPU cable plugged into their GPU rather than the 8 pin Pcie cable you're supposed to use. this can cause a whole bunch of issues, including what we were seeing here with the system not turning on. after rewiring the entire case to make sure everything was connected properly, I turned on the system where I actually got the exact same issue again. at that point I pulled out my Thermaltake Doctor Power 2 Power Supply tester, which I connected to the 24 pin cable and with no response from the power supply, this meant the unit was likely dead. luckily the customer when they brought in the PC already purchased another power supply, so I swapped that out and after doing that the system booted right into the BIOS. so it looks like the issues stem from a dead power supply and incorrect wiring. with these issues now fixed, we can get the PC back to the customer.
