A customer just brought in this Lenovo laptop saying that after dropping the system, the fans started making a loud noise. So let's get a fix for them. First up, after firing up the system for the first time, I was able to hear that there was a clear vibration that was coming from the fans inside of the system, which was likely a result of the drop. So let's get this fixed by first removing the bottom cover and then disconnecting the internal battery to make sure that the system has no power running through it while we work on the system. With the bottom cover off the system, I decided to make sure that the noise was actually coming from the fans first, so I fired up the system without the bottom cover using the power cord. After doing a quick check, I was able to see that a lot of vibrations were clearly coming from the fans. Knowing this information now, I then removed the 6 screws holding in the 2 fans and then I was able to actually unplug and pull those right out. After that I took these 2 brand new fans and got them installed and plugged in. Then because we already had the system apart, I wanted to go ahead and replace the CPU as the system is already approaching a few years old now and I might as well go ahead and do that as we have it opened up to do This. I removed the 3 screws that were holding in the heat sink and pulled that off and got the heat sink and CPU cleaned up with some isopropyl and replaced the CPU with my go to Arctic MX 6 Thermal paste. From there I put the system back together and fired up the system to see that the fan, even at full speed, was significantly quieter and sounded way better than before. So with this system now fixed, we can now get it back to the customer.
