A customer just brought in these old laptops and said they need the drives out so they can donate their laptops without worrying about their data. So let's get these torn apart and the drives out. First up is the Sony Vial, which is a pretty simple system to get the drive out, as all you have to do is disconnect the battery and then remove the 2 screws to access the drive cage. Once you can see the drive cage, there's only one screw left, and after that's removed, you can slide the drive cage right out of the system and then remove the drive from the cage. Next we have this Lenovo Yoga 710, which for this one, you have to remove 10 T5 torx screws and then remove the back cover from the system where you can see all the components. At that point, you can disconnect the battery and then unscrew the M 2 drive. Lastly, we have this Dell Inspiron 14, which is probably one of the easiest systems to access the drive, and then after doing that, just remove one screw from the cover plate and then you can slide the drive right out.
