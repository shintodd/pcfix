can you fry an egg using a CPU? well, in order to find out, I took this aluminum block, stuck it on my CPU with some thermal paste, and then stuck a tiny egg pan on top of that using some more thermal paste. then I fired up a CPU stress test, letting the pan get as warm as possible for about 10 minutes. at that point I threw the egg in the pan to let it fry and after about 25 minutes egg end up cooking perfectly if you like your eggs sunny side up. so it turns out you can fry an egg with a CPU, it just might take a little bit of extra time.
