This lenova laptop is a case of a failed DC Jack. And how I know this is that this laptop just came in not powering on. And when you plug it in, the charging indicator light also doesn't work while pulling absolutely no power from the wall. What gave this away even more was how loose the charge port was when plugging in it. So to go and fix this failed DC Jack, we first need to go and remove the bottom cover off the laptop and then pull out the battery. And after a quick inspection of the motherboard, it looks like we have a soldered port on our hands. So the entire motherboard actually has to be removed out of the system. Once that's out of the way, we can now take our soldering iron and add a little extra solder to each of the actual mounting points for the DC Jack to help with the removal process. And then we can go ahead and fire up our hot air station to go and melt all the solder and pop out the old DC Jack. And after quickly cleaning off the mounting points, can now install our brand new Jack with some fresh sonic. With a new DC Jack installed, we can now reassem the laptop the exact same way that we took it apart. And after plugging in, we now have a working port, making this a working laptop. We can now get back to our customer.
