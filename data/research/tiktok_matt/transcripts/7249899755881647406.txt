A customer just brought in this HP Omen gaming PC and they said the system is running really loud and has started to slow down, so let's get it fixed for them. After firing up the system for the first time, I noticed that the pump was making a ton of noise, way more than it probably should. And after opening up hardware monitor to go ahead and check the system temperatures, the system was idling well above 90 degrees Celsius. So the first thing I wanted to do in this circumstance was to actually remove the cooler and try repasting the CPU. And after taking off the pump block, the Thermal paste was almost completely gone. So I went ahead and repased the system with some Arctic MX 6 which did drop temperatures, but the system was still really loud and it was sitting around 80 degrees Celsius at idle, which is still too warm. So at this point I figured that we must have a failing pump on our hand. So I took this newer version of the Intermax cooler that was already installed in the system and got that installed and after doing so the system was much quieter and idle temperatures were down into the 30s, which was way better than it was before. So after all, it looks like the cooler did have a bad pump, as the system was really struggling to actually keep the system cool even with the new Thermal paste and With this system now fixed, all we have to do is get it cleaned up because it's absolutely filthy. And after doing that, we can now get the system back to the customer.
