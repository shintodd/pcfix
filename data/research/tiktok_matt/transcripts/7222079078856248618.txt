This is the filthiest GPU I think I've ever seen, so let's take it off back and get it cleaned up. Using the data vac, which is an incredibly powerful duster, I was able to clear out most of the clumps of dust, but the card was still pretty dirty. So I then took some isopropyl alcohol that imported over the card to break down some of the remaining dust and then blew it out again. Surprisingly, after doing all of this there was still a bunch of clumps of dust remaining, so I then took some tweezers to pull out the rest. With all I complete, the card looks way better than it did before and I can now plug it in for testing without worrying about starting a dust storm in my office.
