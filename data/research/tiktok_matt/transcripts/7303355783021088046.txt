This gaming PC is stuck in a constant loop of showing that the BIOS has been reset on every single restart. And I noticed after entering the BIOS that the time is off. This is almost a sure indication that you have a failed CMOS battery if you ever see this. But in order to verify this, I went ahead and remove the CMOS battery and then use my battery tester to go and verify that this battery was in fact dead. When this battery dies and your system loses power, there's no way for your system to actually remember any of the BIOS or time settings, thus causing this repetitive loop. After replacing the CR 2032 battery on the system's motherboard, the system is now firing up and no longer getting stuck on that air.
