This might be the future of power supplies. This Corsairs Shift 08:50 X and this powers play is something completely different about it. That's the fact that instead of having the modular interface on the opposite side of the power connector like most other modular power supplies, instead this one has them coming out of the side. And why this even matters is the fact that by having it like this, it makes upgrading and adding additional power cables to your PC significantly easier. And it also shrinks to overall footprint when you're in smaller cases, making it way easier if you don't have a ton of room between the power supply and hard drive gauge. I personally really like this shift and hope to see this implemented by more power supply manufacturers moving forward.
