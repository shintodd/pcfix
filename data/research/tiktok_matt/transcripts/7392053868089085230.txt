This old and dirty cobweb filled PC just came into my shop because it stopped displaying anything out and the second I plugged it in I instantly had an idea of exactly what was going on here. See, the system would just turn on as soon as you plugged in power without you pressing the power switch. And by powering up I would just mean the fans would turn on and then never slow down, looking like the system was failing. The post. This is typically a sign of a bad motherboard, but to verify this I first reset the CMOS, then check the power button wiring, followed by checking the system memory and even tested with a known good CPU where every time the system would just go back to that same no display and start up on its own. This LED me to conclude that in fact I was right about the bad motherboard. Unfortunately DEL likes to use proprietary form factors and getting a replacement board wasn't worth it for a customer. But they do need one folder off the old computer that holds all of their Quickbooks information. Wish to go in, access and back this up for our customer? We first pulled out their SSD and slid it into our Oracle drive slide and then plug that into another PC along with a flash driver battery backup. After finding their desired Quickbooks folder on their desktop, I copied it over to the flash drive and Just like that, their data is now safe and it can be moved over to their brand new computer.
