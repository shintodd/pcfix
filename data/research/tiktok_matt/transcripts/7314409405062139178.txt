Can this 2 inch cube of solid aluminum cool CPU? Well, let's find out. After booting up the system, we saw that under no load the CPU was running quite cool, sitting around 30 degrees Celsius. In order to get a better idea of how well it would perform, we ran the CPU stress test, which it held up way better than I would have expected, lasting about 7 minutes before it reached 100 degrees Celsius, causing the CPU to start thermalthrogling.
