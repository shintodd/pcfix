a viewer asked can you make popcorn with the CPU? well, in order to find out, I took a copper cup, lined the bottom with popcorn kernels, and stuck it on my CPU with some thermal paste. then after firing up the PC, I ran a CPU stress test where unfortunately after 25 minutes the copper cup wasn't getting hot enough to make the kernels pop. which, after doing some further research, looks like they actually need to reach 180 degrees Celsius to actually start popping, which is far hotter than what the CPU will ever get to. so it turns out you can't pop popcorn with a CPU. but what should we try next to cook with the CPU? let me know in the comments down below.
