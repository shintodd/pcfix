Customer just brought in this laptop. As they said the system has been running slow and they wanted to upgrade the system to a solid state drive. So let's get it upgraded. In order to do this, we first need to remove the bottom cover off the laptop. At that point, the battery then can be unscrewed and removed. From there, the old drive then can be pulled up and the new SSD from Sandis can be connected and installed. After putting the system back together the same way it was taken apart, all that has to be done now is a clean installation of Windows as requested by the customer. And with that now complete, we can now get this upgraded laptop back to the customer.
