If you have a dead computer that you want to recover data from, one way you can do that is to get a drive dock like this one from Star Tech. What this allows you to do is actually extract your hold hard drive from inside of your laptop or desktop and then connect it over to the drive dock and then plug that into any other computer. And after powering up the dock, the drive should pop up right in the windows, allowing you to navigate to your user folder, where you can access and copy all of your files wherever you may need to. Personally, I use one of these in my computer repair shop every single day, and it's allowed me to save data for hundreds if not thousands of PCs over the years.
