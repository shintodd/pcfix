A customer just brought in their gaming PC and they said they were trying to upgrade it, but the system's no longer turning on. So let's get it fixed for them. After doing a quick test, we can see that the system seems to be stuck on the DRAM LED, which generally indicates an issue with RAM. And after doing a quick look I noticed the sticks of RAM were in slots one and 3 instead of the generally recommended 2 and 4. So I went to go ahead and swap these out and notice that the stick and slot one was kind of loose, so I reinserting the RAM in its proper location. As soon as that was done, the system fired right up in the window. So it looks like the issue was just with the loose RAM. And with the system now back up and running, we can now get it back to the customer.
