a guy just posted on a forum that he upgraded his CPU and motherboard and now all of a sudden the PC stuck in a loop where the diagnostic LEDs just go from the CPU to D RAM to VGA to boot over and over and over again as the system is kind of like restarting each time. And I believe that there are three possibilities of why the system isn't functioning the way he would expect it to. First up, his RAM configuration is not in the ideal setup. He's got his sticks in slots 1 and 3 rather than 2 and 4, if you check the motherboard manual for this particular motherboard, is recommended to be in 2 and 4, and although it doesn't have to be, some motherboards can be a little picky. Second, he says that the BIOS has been updated, but because it's a B FOUR FIFTYF gaming motherboard with an AMD Ryzen 5 50 600G, there's still a possibility. So I would definitely check the BIOS before proceeding with anything. Make sure that is up to date. If you have an older CPU, use that to update it. If it has a quick flash button, use that. And then lastly, what's most likely the issue is there's no CPU cooler on the CPU. That will cause the system to restart over and over again because it's overheating instantly as it's trying to fire up, which will cause the system just not to function. So Throw a CPU cooler on there
