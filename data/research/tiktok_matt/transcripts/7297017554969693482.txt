The other day I exposed this 2 terabyte scam external SSD on Amazon for being a glorified microsd card reader with just a 64 gigabyte microsd card inside. And I had a number of comments on this video as well. Some other scams that I've exposed in the past to see what would happen if we put a normal micro SD card into this fake SSD. And I was honestly curious about this too, so I went ahead and did so to find out that it just registered like a normal SD card reader. Which, based on my experience where I had the scam SD card that actually acted like a normal 64 gigabyte card when it was plugged into a different SD card reader, this means that the trickery that these scammers are actually running isn't just controlled by the card reader itself and this controller that I thought originally controlled this functionality, but rather it's a combination of both the controller as well as the actual SD card firmware to allow it to actually show up as a scam where it shows as two terabytes instead of its real 64 gigabytes. So once again, as I've mentioned plenty of times before, anytime you buy any kind of media online, check the manufacture and if the deal is too good to be true like this one, it probably is.
