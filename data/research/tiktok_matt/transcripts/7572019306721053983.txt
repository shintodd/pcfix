If you ever notice that your GPU fans aren't spinning, don't panic because more than likely nothing's actually wrong. As almost every modern GPU cooler design these days is designed for and shipped with a zero RPM mode on the manufacturer, it may also be referred to as a zero Death simple mode. Regardless, just as the name implies, these modern GPUs have the ability to cut off their fans completely as long as the GPU temperature is under a certain threshold. Often when your system's idling, doing any task that's not GPU intensive, where it's not going to produce Enough heat that the built in heat sink can't passively dissipate using the airflow from the rest of the case without having to kick on these fans. And this is all done by design for one main benefit, which with two or three fans not having to run in your Case means your GPU can operate in complete silence, reducing the overall noise level of your entire system. At least until you start running something GPU demanding, where your temperatures start to increase, where those fans have to kick on. So just know if you ever notice your GPU fans Aren't running while you're idling. Well, it means they're likely running in a zero RPM mode.
