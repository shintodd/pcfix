This is a five pound bar of copper. Let's see if it can cool a CPU. In order to test this out, we strapped it using a massive zip tie to our test bench, which featured an I 7 87 under K. After firing up our test bench, the copper bar did a surprisingly good job at keeping temperatures under control, especially idle with the CPU sitting around 26 to 28 degrees Celsius. Ever to understand its true capabilities, I fired up a full CPU stress test in which the bar of copper was actually able to keep the CPU under normal temperatures for nearly five minutes before the CPU started overheat and Thermal throttle.
