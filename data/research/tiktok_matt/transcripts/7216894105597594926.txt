The customer just brought in this HP laptop wanting to upgrade it to an SSD, but didn't want to lose any data. So let's get it upgraded for them. In order to make this happen, we're going to have to clone the existing hard drive over to the new SSD as an exact mirror image of the old. To do this, we first had to remove the battery off the laptop and then remove the back cover. With that drive now exposed, it can be unplugged and then plugged into our star tech drive cloner with the brand new SSD from crucial. After letting that drive clone over to the new SSD, we were able to get it back installed in the system and reassemble laptop. Now with that new SSD installed and the data cloned over, we can now get this system back to the customer.
