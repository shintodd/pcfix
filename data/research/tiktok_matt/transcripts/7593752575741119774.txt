Thermal takes trying to attack the retro style market that Silverstone's really been pushing for lately. But I'll be completely honest, I am not a fan of how they did it cause even though the AIO is kind of cool inside, the rest of the case doesn't feel all that nice. Looks cheap if I had to say anything as the concept is cool, but the implementation I just don't think is as well done. Especially when you consider the front fascia. Just doesn't seem as nice as some of the others I've seen. Especially if you consider this the Silverstone one. And outside of the just front plastic and the color of the inside of the case, there's really nothing old or retro about this case. It feels more like this piece was more of an afterthought than anything. Especially when you consider they missed the point to be able to put a five and a quarter inch bay, as this is actually fake. Which you'll see because if you look on the backside of the case, there's no bay there at all. it's just a little plastic cover that you could replace with a screen. Which they have on their retro 2 60 version. Which I think I'm actually more of a fan of, but I still got a lot of questions of why you would want an LCD screen on the front of your retro looking case. Just a little confusing for me.
