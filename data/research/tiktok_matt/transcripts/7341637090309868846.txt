Sometimes, despite PCs being completely repairable, customers choose other options due to cost and their needs. For instance, this HP Elite Book doesn't power on, and I'm pretty sure it's battery related, but the customer brought the system in just requesting that the data be extracted. To do this, it's quite easy, especially when you consider the repair friendly nature of this laptop design is it only requires one latch to go and lift up the bottom cover, exposing the battery, RAM, and hard drive. From there we can then undo two more latches to go and pop out the battery and then we can unscrew and slide out the SSD from inside the laptop. That drive now out, all we got to do is plug that into a Star Tag Drive dock and from there we can go and connect that over to another PC along with the USB flash drive that will be using to place all the recovered files on. From there we can then navigate to the customer's user folder and select the user files that were requested by the customer and copy and paste them over onto the flash drive. And just like that, the data is now backed up. And even though the system was never repaired, the data is safe and our customers happy. So that's all that matters.
