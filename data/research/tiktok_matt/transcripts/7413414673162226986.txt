The PS five Pro is nowhere near the same level of performance of an RTX 40 80. It's honestly not even close. But for some reason a bunch of people think that this is true, I think it's just because of how Sony was marketing the PS five Pro and that it can run certain games at 4K, 60 frames per second, or even 120 frames per second. Which sounds incredible and would technically beat an RTX 40 80 until you understand what they were talking about. Because Sony introduced their PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution, which is just an AI upscaler very similar to DLS or FSR. Which means when they're talking about those games running at those faster frames per second, it's actually just a lower resolution being rendered and then upscaling, which you can do on any modern GPU these days. And if you're to do the same thing with the RTX 40 80 with DLSS frame generation and super resolution turned on, it would absolutely destroy the console. Probably double the frames per second at 4K. And even if Sony was able to match the performance of an RTX 40 80, which they don't, it's still up to the developers and Sony working together to create enhanced games that allow for this technology to take place. Which we still have to see how well that development actually works out.
