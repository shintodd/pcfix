This Alienware gaming PC has a really weird issue that's causing it to get stuck on the Alienware logo on every single startup and it won't proceed any further. So to figure out what was going on, I first wanted to see if maybe we could try to boot into the BIOS. However, the system would freeze each time on a logo, not even allowing you to press F 2 or F 12 to enter the BIOS or pause the startup process from there. I was actually pretty certain that we might be dealing with a BIOS issue, as sometimes resetting it can actually take care of these issues. And because this board doesn't have a quick BIOS reset, I went ahead and popped out the CMOS battery out of the motherboard and then installed it back onto the board and then reset the BIOS settings and after turning the system back on we were back to the exact same issue. From there I then tried swapping out the RAM which ended up making no difference. And then I finally went to go ahead and swap out the GPU and after pulling out the old GPU and putting my tester card into the system, it fired right up into the BIOS. Just to make sure that this was actually the GPU that was causing the issue, not that the card either came loose or there was a wiring issue, I went ahead and tried reinstalling the old card back into The system where we ended up getting no change. So with this said, it looks like we're just dealing with a bad GPU, and we can now let the customer know that they're going to need to get that replaced.
