This workstation has one of the weirdest issues where it will turn on perfectly fine, but then after you reboot it or turn it on a second time, no matter what you do, the system fails to display anything out. Really weird, right? Well it actually gets weirder as the diagnostic LED that gets hung on this MSI motherboard is the GPU LED. Which is kind of odd because we are running directly on internal graphics which usually don't hang there. But after checking out some forum posts, this LED sometimes will get flagged from a bad drive, and because there are eight in the system plus two DVD drives, there's a high chance of that being the case. I started by disconnecting all the drives except for the boot drive just to go ahead and check, but we got the same issue. A single boot would work and then we got stuck on that GPU LED again. So I pulled power and the data connection from that boot drive and the system loaded fine over and over again. Well, looks like the issue stems from the Samsung 8 70 EVO drive, which happens to be the newest one in the system. And I also even verified this by checking a different state of power and data connection, but there was no difference. So because it looks like this drive is failing, our customer said just to go and clone it to a brand new drive we can install This much faster. T500 drive from crucial. Then reconnect the bad hard drive, plug in a USB of the Cronus True Image to go and create a 1 to 1 mirror copy of all the data. And after letting that run, it finished successfully. And we can go and unplug that drive. And this system now boots up fine over and over again. And it's even a little faster thanks to the M dot 2 drive.
