Here's how I was able to turn on my PC with this toggle switch. First, you need six things a way to cut and strip wire, a screwdriver, a PC power switch, two pin cable, a momentary toggle switch, a cover, and lastly a plate or location. You plan them out. The hardware to start. First, cut the power switch cable as close as you can to the power button, then strip the wires about a half inch back. Next, loosen the screws on the toggle switch itself and wrap the stripped wire around them and tighten it back down. Next, loosen the collar around the switch and place the switch in the mounting place. Then place the cover over the switch and place the color back on. Lastly, plug in your two pin header into the front panel connectors on your motherboard and then you can turn your PC on with the toggle switch.
