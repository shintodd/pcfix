You know the Intel Inside stickers that come on prebuilds laptops and in every new CPU box that you buy? Well, did you know that if you ever rip, tear, or even misplace your Intel in side stickers, Intel will actually send you a replacement? It's called the Intel Processor Label Replacement Request Program, and basically all you have to do is submit proof of your original purchase and explain what happened to the original sticker. And after testing this out for myself, 3 days later a package showed up at my door with a brand new sticker.
