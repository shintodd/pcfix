This PC accessory allows you to add RGB to any PC case fan. It's called the Fantex Halo Frame and it works by mounting to the outside of a fan using 4 included screws and can be controlled either by using the built in lighting controller in most Fantex cases or with a special adapter. It can be controlled using your motherboard's built in RGB controller. At only 06:54 dollars depending on the size you need, the Halo frame is a great way to add RGB lighting to your build at a reasonable price.
