A customer just brought in their old Dell XPS and they wanted to know the best upgrade option is. They didn't want to replace the entire system for another year. So after a quick evaluation we decided on upgrading the old mechanical hard drive to a solid state drive as this would make a big impact to most stated day task. To do this I pulled out the old drive, cloned it to this 1 terabyte SSD from crucial, and installed the new drive in the system. After doing this we fired up the system. We were able to drop boot times down from 39 seconds down to 22 seconds, and the PC in general seemed to be running way smoother. So it looks like we can get this upgraded system back to the customer.
