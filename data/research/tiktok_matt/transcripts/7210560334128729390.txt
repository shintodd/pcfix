These are by far the coolest PC case fans you can buy right now. These are the Thermaltake Suavan EX and they snap together using magnets to transmit both data and power over metal contact points using a single cable. This allows you to reduce unnecessary cable clutter and makes the planning process for your build so much simpler. On top of the magnetic feature, these fans support great RGB implementation and include reversible fan blades depending on the orientation you might need. Overall, I really like what Thermaltake has been able to do with these fans, and I hope to see others in the industry adopt the same magnetic technology in the future.
