can you cool a CPU using a cup of ice? well, in order to find out, I took a copper cup, wrapped it in a towel to absorb any extra moisture, and stuck it on my CPU with some thermal paint. once it was complete, I added an ice cube and fired up the system in the windows. after adding a couple extra ice cubes to the cup, the CPU was idling around 8 to 10 degrees Celsius. so in order to really put this to the test, I fired up a CPU stress test where it ran for over 10 minutes as I slowly added more ice as needed. by doing this, the CPU ended up settling around 48 degrees C. So it turns out you can cool a CPU with a cup of ice. what should we try next to cool a CPU? let me know in the comments down below.
