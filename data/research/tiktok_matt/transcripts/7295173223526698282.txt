This right here is the best laptop cooling pad that money can buy. This is the Lano Laptop Cooling Pad, and this cooler follows a very similar design to the IETS GT500 V2, which I previously named the best laptop cooling pad on the market. But due to its massive 28 RPM fan, this cooler is able to drop temperatures on this older Asus gaming laptop over 20 degrees Celsius idle. And it dropped temperatures under a full load stress test by nearly 29%, or 24 degrees Celsius to be exact. This is 7 degrees better than even the IETS and swept the floor with every other laptop cooler that I tested this against. With that said, there are three big drawbacks when it comes to this cooler. First, the fact that it does require external power from a traditional wall outlet, meaning that portability for this cooler is hurt. Second is the price, which is a hundred and nine dollars and ninety nine cents, which is pretty insane for a laptop cooler. And lastly is the noise, which might just make you go insane.
