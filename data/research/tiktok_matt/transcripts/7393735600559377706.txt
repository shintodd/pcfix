Market warranty companies are the biggest PC scam. See, this MSI laptop just came into my shop with a double hinge displacement where both hinges ripped right off the assembly. And normally I would go after the poor design from MSI here, which definitely does exist, but in the case of this system, it was just fixed a couple months back by Square Trade, an aftermarket Protection plant company who repaired a broken hinge previously. But from my findings, it doesn't look like they knew anything what they were doing, because not only did they break the customer's bezel in the process, but it also looks like they just glued back the metal inserts in the place and called it a day and hoped it would work. Well, let me tell you, this is never the right fix because it LED to the same side breaking, followed by putting additional stress on the other side, causing that to also break. The proper fix for this type of situation is to actually replace the back panel assembly, which is exactly what we did here, and swap out the broken bezel, which transformed this falling apart PC to something that can finally be used for years to come.
