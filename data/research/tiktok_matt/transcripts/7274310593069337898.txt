This laptop cooler sucks. Literally. This is a vacuum laptop cooler from climb, and unlike most other laptop cooling pads that we've tested before, this one's super unique as instead of pushing air through the bottom of your laptop, this one actually sucks all the hot air out of your side exhaust. And after running some tests with his old Asus gaming laptop, we were able to drop CPU temperatures at idle by 13 degrees Celsius, an 8 degree Celsius under a full load stress test. Which I gotta say, is quite impressive for such a small portable device. But like most high power coolers on the market, this one will definitely come at the expense of your ears.
