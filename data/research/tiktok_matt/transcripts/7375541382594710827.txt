Not all gaming PCs show up in great shape at my shop, and it's usually a result where the customer wanted to upgrade their PC but something goes terribly wrong. Which is the case with this system where the CPU cooler was just upgraded, but after booting the system up, system now shows a CPU fan air and instantly overheats to 100 degrees Celsius at idle. Well, anytime I see something like this, it's a pretty sure shot that the pump was probably left unplugged. And although the sense cable was plugged into the motherboard, I noticed that the pump was also not lighting up at all. So after digging through the rat's nest of cables, it looks like the Commander Pro that drives the fans and pump on this cooler was connected properly to everything except for power. So after plugging in the Sega connection between the Commander Pro and our power supply, the system now boots up without a CPU fan air. And after checking temps and windows, we went from 100 degree Celsius down to the mid thirties. Although I would have definitely like to clean up this build, my customer didn't bring half of the required mounting hardware and their additional fans, so they said they just wanted to go and wrap it up at home. Which is perfectly fine with me. So let's go ahead and get this system back over to them.
