The GTX 1080 Ti is the real GOAT of GPUs. This is one of the most impressive cards to ever be released for gamers because not only did it come in one of the most iconic reference cooler designs that had this really cool angular design that was really cool looking, but it was an amazing performance. Because honestly, compared to the current cards out there on the market, there was really two cards that I had to compete with, the GTX 1080 and the Titan XP. And compared to those two cards, the GTX 1080 Ti was about 30 to 35% faster than the previous release 1080 and it was on par with the Titan XP. But the best part about this card was it was released at just 699 compared to 1199 for that Titan card, making this an amazing value. I just feel like we'll never see again. And that's why I gotta call it the GOAT of GPUs.
