This laptop just came to my shop as my customer said that they were trying to do a clean install windows on their system, and they end up with this error. Basically, the system failed to finish the installation and wants you to start over. So let's get this system fixed. First off, I wanted to go ahead and try my own Windows installation media and my USB flash drive just to be safe. And after doing so, it didn't make any kind of change. So at that point I knew some kind of hardware was likely preventing the install from taking place. But to verify what wasn't working properly, I shut the system off and restarted it. While firing the system up, I pressed F12, which allowed me to go ahead and select diagnostic mode, which basically runs a built in diagnostic utility on Dell laptops. And although all the system components actually checked out and came back clean, I noticed something that was really weird. The battery was completely dead and had no charging flow. So? In most cases, systems can function perfectly fine without their battery working. Others are somewhat dependent on that battery being functional. So I flipped over the system and pulled out the battery and put a brand new replacement in. And after firing the system up, I reran the Windows installation media, and guess what? This time it went right through. So it looks like somehow the battery was throwing air. That was Significant enough to prevent the reinsulation of Windows. So, with the system now functional once again and charging normally, we can now get this laptop back to our customer.
