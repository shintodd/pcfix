This is an Nvidia GTX 7 50 Ti released back in 2014. This was the ultimate budget graphics card for gaming. What made this GPU such a popular option for budget gamers was the fact that the card at the time could run most games at 1080 p at no problem. And because most models were so power efficient, they drew less than 60 Watts, meaning they could actually run off just the PCI power and didn't need any additional power cables, making the perfect to turn any off the shelf PC that you could buy at any store into a fully capable gaming system. And not to mention these cards only sold for $150.
