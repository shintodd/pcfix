hey Zach, if this PC came in my shop, likely the first thing I would be checking for would be power. anytime you see fans not spinning or lights not turning on, usually it's related to power. these cases also usually come with a fan hub, so I'd be checking the SATA connector there where most of the time they just either come loose. or try with a different SATA connector coming off your power supply. if you're still having the same issue, it might be a power supply issue. so you can either try with the friends or take it to a local shop. they usually have a spare power supply you can test with.
