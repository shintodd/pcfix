Something that I think laptop manufacturers really need to bring back is quick swappable batteries, as many older systems had the ability to within seconds to replace your laptop battery with a brand new one. Which compared to today, some of the worst may take removing 10 plus screws and then carefully prying the laptop apart before being able to even see the battery, let alone remove it. Which honestly is just bad for consumers as it often prevents them from being able to replace their batteries themselves, as nearly every laptop battery will eventually fail, requiring them to have to actually do tons of research to possibly do it themselves. Or to have to bring it into a shop like mine, which really just isn't necessary.
