This HP laptop's owner is completely locked out of their device because they accidentally set a bit locker under an account they no longer have access to, thus giving them no way to go ahead and access to recovery key. And a case like this, the only way to really get the system to be usable once again is to do a clean installation of Windows. To do this, we first got to go ahead and plug in our USB installation media, and then on start up, we can press escape and then F9 to open up the boot menu. There we can then select our USB drive as our boot device. Once we're booted onto the USB drive, we can now follow the prompts to go and reform at the installed drive and get Windows Reloaded on the machine. And with the system now reset and now usable once again, we can now get it back to the customer.
