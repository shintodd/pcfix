Can you install a GPU cooler in a CPU? Let's find out. In order to try this, I took a GT7 40 from EVGA due to its small form factor. Remove the cooler from the car, then I took the removed cooler, plugged in the fan connector to the motherboard's fan header, and placed the cooler on top of the CPU with some Thermal paste. Once it was complete, we booted up the system and ended up working just fine with idle temperatures sitting in the mid 30s. So it turns out you can install a GPU cooler on a CPU.
