I just bought this R9280 on eBay for an upcoming video and it ended up having a bad fan that was making this really loud rattling noise. so in order to fix this I first wanted to see if just cleaning off the fan would do the trick, but after trying it was still making the same noise. so at that point I decided it was just best to replace it. to do this I took the card completely apart, removed the 2 stock fans and installed the new ones. once that was complete I repasted the card and put it back together and as you can hear now the card is back to normal.
