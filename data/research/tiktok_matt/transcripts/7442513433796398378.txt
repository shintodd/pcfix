So many people neglect maintenance on their PC and it results in damaged fans and eventually high temperatures. And maintaining your PC is not that hard. Just pick up something like a data back or small portable air duster. It's as simple as taking your PC outside, sticking your fingers in the fan blades, and blowing and repeat the process with the rest of the components. Just like that, the PC is now cleaned up and looking great. But let's take it a step further, because by pulling off this fan, doing these screws allows us to pop the CPU cooler right off, revealing some dried up Thermal paste. Then by taking some isopropyl alcohol on a paper towel, we can take all of that old Thermal paste right off. If we take some fresh Thermal paste, get that on the CPU, reinstall the cooler, and now not only is this PC clean, but it's also running cooler thanks to that repaste job and no longer having those suffocated fans. Maintenance on your PC isn't hard. Just about anybody can do it. It's just neglected to watch.
