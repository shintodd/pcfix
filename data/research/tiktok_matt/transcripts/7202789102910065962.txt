A customer just brought in the Stele laptop which had said after replacing the laptop battery they were getting a no boot device found air message. So after testing this for myself to confirm the issue, I figured that it was likely something wasn't put back correctly during the install. In order to fix this, I took the system apart to see if there were any noticeable issues were surprising. I noticed the issue immediately as the M. 2 drive wasn't installed correctly. So I unscrewed the drive and then installed it back directly and put the system back together. Then I fired up the system to see that loaded right into Windows without an issue. So with the laptop now fixed, we can now get it back to the customer.
