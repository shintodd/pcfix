can you cook pasta using a CPU? well, in order to find out, I took a copper cup and placed it on my CPU with some thermal paste and filled it up halfway with water. after doing that I fired up the system and ran a CPU stress test until the system started to overheat to get the water warm. at that point I took some angel hair pasta, broke it in half, and threw it in the cup. after letting the CPU cook the pasta for about 25 to 30 minutes, it came out perfect. so it turns out that you can cook pasta with the CPU, which I try next to cook with the CPU. let me know in the comments down below.
