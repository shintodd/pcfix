One of the most exciting cases here at Coffeetex is this BTF GPU concept case from Cooler Master. The reason for this is they are able to fit a 40 90 and probably one of the smallest cases I've seen. And this is actually all thanks to a special connector, the BTF connector. Working in collaboration with Asus, they are able to convert it to a 12 + 4 pin connector, which is super cool. And it's really cool because if you actually twist this, you can actually pull the GPU out just like that and you can see the connector that they're using and it basically sits on this flat base with the actual PCI connector as well as the BTF connector. It is really impressive and something I'm a huge fan of here at Computex.
