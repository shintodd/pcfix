A customer just brought in this custom watercooled gaming PC and they said that the system started overheating recently and shut down, so let's get it fixed for them. After turning on the system for the first time, I noticed that the pump actually wasn't running, and you could tell this by the flow indicator that was in the distribution block wasn't spinning. So it's possible that we have a bad pump on our hands, but just to be safe, I wanted to make sure that it wasn't something else causing this, like a loose cable. After opening up the back panel, which had one of the most filthy dust filters I've ever seen, I tracked down and pulled out the Molex connector that powers the pump and I noticed it wasn't fully secure, so I re plugged that in after going to fire the system back up. Wouldn't you know what? The pump fired up without an issue. So it looks like the system just had a loose Molex connector for the pump, and we can now get the system cleaned up as it's absolutely filthy. And this dust filter here is probably the worst one I've ever seen. As I mentioned earlier, and with this system now all cleaned up, running smoothly in the pump working, we can now get it back to the customer.
