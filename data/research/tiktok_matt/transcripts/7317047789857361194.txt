I pretty much never recommend anyone buying all on one desktop due to their lack of repairability, overheating issues, and often slower hardware that you get compared to a traditional desktop due to power and Thermal limitations of most of the designs. However, if you're gonna get one, you need to make sure it has this one feature. That said, not only has an HDMI out, but also an HDMI in. This is input port allows you to connect any computer, console, or any other kind of HDMI outputting device to the built in display, meaning that when you're still using the computer, you can have a second device hooked up to the display like a laptop. But what's even better is when this all in one eventually stops working or is no longer worth upgrading anymore, you can actually still use the display of the all in one computer or another system, or any kind of other HDMI device in the future, keeping the system from being erased any sooner than it has to be. But at the same time, keep in mind that you're never gonna beat the flexibility of a traditional desktop and monitor setup.
