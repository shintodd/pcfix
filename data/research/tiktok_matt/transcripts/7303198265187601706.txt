Have you ever struggled to plug in a USB device in the back of your motherboard or on your laptop? Well, let me tell you, there's a standard you should be following. According to the USB standard, the female connector should always have the pins on the top. This means when you're plugging in a USB device with the mail connector, they should be on the bottom. Here's an example of what I mean with a couple IO Shields as well as a laptop that shows this exact same orientation. So next time you're plugging in a USB device, just keep the pins down and you should be good to go.
