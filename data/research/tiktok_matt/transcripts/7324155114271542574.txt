This is Thermaltake's Tower 300 case, and it's probably my new favorite case out there on the market as it takes all my favorite aspects of the Tower 200, but also increases it slightly in size by becoming more octagonal in shape with more viewing area of your component. They also left one of my favorite features of all the Tower Series cases, which is the number of colors that you could choose from, including black, white, hydrania blue, matcha green, racing green, turquoise, or bumblebee. And what I'm looking forward to more than anything with this case is a really cool stand they were showing off which allows you to lay your case on its side at an angle, allowing you to really showcase all the components inside of your system. And it to me it almost looks like a cryo chamber, which is just so cool.
