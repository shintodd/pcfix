I'm certain this external SSD is a total scam, as it claims to be a 2 terabyte external SSD for only around $50 over on Amazon. So I went ahead and bought this drive to go and expose it because the pricing is just way too good to be true. So to determine what's going on with this drive, I first connected it to a PC to see that. The drive did show up as a two deer by drive and File Explorer, and it was able to pass a reformat test, which most of these scam drives actually fail. But when I try to go ahead and do a crystal discmark run, the speeds were absolutely terrible, slower than most USB 2.0 flash drives. I knew something was going on here. So I went ahead and popped open the drive to see that this was not an SSD. In fact, rather I just a glorified micro SD card reader with a micro SD card inside. And based on my experience, I doubted this is an actual 2 terabyte micro SD card. So after cutting out the weirdly placed hot glue which might have been used either to try to add some weight or just to try to keep it closed, I was actually went ahead and plug that SD card into a normal USB card reader, where I was able to go ahead and see that that drive wasn't even formated. And when I wanted to go ahead and Format it myself. It was only a 64 gigabyte drive. So this is a perfect example of a scam on Amazon. Luckily I was able to go ahead and report the listing and I got it taken down. But please beware when buying any kind of storage online. As if you ever see something that's too good to be true, it likely is, like in this case.
