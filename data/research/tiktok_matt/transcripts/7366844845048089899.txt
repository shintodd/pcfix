This laptop can't stop coughing, and I honestly can't blame it because if I was suffocating in dust like this fan is, I probably would be too. it doesn't help when your CPU's Thermal paste is partially dried up, which prevents proper Thermal transfer, and you have a clogged heat sink. It just makes the already struggling fan have to run that much harder. Fortunately for this system, it just needs a little bit of love and it'll be back up and running like new. And by love I mean a good cleaning, including playing my favorite instrument, the heat sink harmonica, and applying some fresh Arctic MXX Thermal paste, which, after applying to the CPU and reassembling the system, made this one's coughing laptop whisper quiet.
